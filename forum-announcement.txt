Drive Placement
A simple, drag-and-drop map of your server's physical drive layout.

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hernandito/Drive.Placement/main/screenshot.png

Drive Placement maps your unRAID drives onto a clean visual layout of your actual case, so the dashboard matches what you see when you open up the server. Each drive sits in a column that represents its real cage, stack, or the motherboard - so you always know which physical bay any drive lives in.

Setup takes about a minute. There's nothing to configure: your drives show up automatically in an Unplaced pool, you drag them into columns that match your hardware, and click Save. No tray definitions, no per-slot forms, no wizard.

Every drive card shows, at a glance:
- Temperature (color-coded) and a "Zzz" marker when a drive is spun down
- Usage bar, free space, size, and filesystem (xfs, btrfs, zfs, ntfs, fat, exfat ...)
- The last four of the serial number - the same short tag you label your physical drives with
- Media type and a category badge (Array, Parity, Pool, Unassigned, Flash)
- A red card for any drive reporting an issue, so problems stand out

More of what's inside:
- Unassigned Devices - full temperature, usage, and free-space readout, a clear "Not mounted" flag, and per-partition sub-lines for multi-partition drives
- Identify a drive - a button blinks the drive's own activity LED so you can find it in the bay
- Compact and full views, light/dark skin aware, with per-category and per-card background colors
- Custom names and notes on any drive
- Printable PDF report of your whole layout
- Layouts saved to your flash drive - they survive reboots and plugin updates

How is this different from Disk Location?
The excellent Disk Location plugin by olehj (https://forums.unraid.net/topic/77302-plugin-disk-location/) is powerful and deeply feature-rich. Drive Placement takes a different tack: dead-simple setup and a clean, easy-to-read design. Set it up in under a minute - no tray/row/column configuration - and read your whole array at a glance. If you want maximum configurability, Disk Location is a great choice. If you want a drive map that takes a minute to set up and is effortless to read, give Drive Placement a try.

Install
Available in Community Applications - search for "Drive Placement". Or install manually via Plugins - Install Plugin:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hernandito/Drive.Placement/main/driveplacement.plg
The dashboard appears under Settings - Utilities - Drive Placement.
